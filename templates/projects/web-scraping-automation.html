The Challenge

Modern target web applications employ aggressive client-side anti-scraping systems, including canvas fingerprinting, WebGL sniffing, CAPTCHA challenges, rate-limiting, and infinite scrolling feeds. Extracting thousands of product listings and business directories required a resilient, self-healing crawler infrastructure that mimics natural human interactions.

Engineering Architecture & Solutions

Stealth Anti-Bot Evasion

Injected stealth evasions stripping navigator.webdriver flags, randomized user-agents, spoofed viewport parameters, and dynamic mouse movement jitter.

Residential Proxy Pools

Automated session-aware proxy routing with automatic retry backoff, backchannel health checks, and circuit-breaker tripping upon 429 rate-limiting.

Automated ETL Data Ingestion

Pipelines sanitize, validate, and deduplicate extracted payloads via Pydantic schemas before syncing to PostgreSQL, AWS S3, or Google Sheets.

Automated Headless CRON Scheduling

Configured automated headless runs on serverless container workers with automatic failure email alerts and Discord webhook updates.
