Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Detection

An advanced computer vision system designed to prevent road accidents caused by driver fatigue. Utilizing OpenCV, MediaPipe Face Mesh, and mathematical landmark calculation (Eye Aspect Ratio - EAR), it monitors eye closure duration and head nodding to trigger sub-second auditory safety alerts.

The Challenge

Drowsy driving accounts for thousands of vehicular fatalities annually. Existing in-vehicle commercial detection hardware is expensive, proprietary, and often fails under fluctuating lighting conditions (night drives, direct sunlight) or when drivers wear spectacles. The objective was to build an ultra-lightweight, hardware-agnostic solution that operates reliably on consumer-grade webcams with zero GPU requirements.

Architecture & Engineering Solution

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Algorithm

Calculates Euclidean distances between vertical and horizontal eye landmark coordinates. If EAR drops below a calibrated threshold for more than 20 consecutive frames, drowsiness is flagged.

Yawn & Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR)

Tracks mouth opening ratio over time to detect frequent yawning patterns as an early warning precursor before micro-sleep episodes occur.

Multi-Level Audio & Visual Alerting

Fires high-decibel audio frequencies through Pygame audio hooks alongside bold red on-screen warning bounding boxes to snap driver attention back to the road.

Low-Compute CPU Optimization

Optimized landmark extraction pipeline using lightweight MediaPipe pipelines to sustain a fluid 30+ FPS video pipeline on basic standard laptop hardware.
