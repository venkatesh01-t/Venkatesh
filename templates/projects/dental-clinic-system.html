Smart Dental Clinic Management System

A comprehensive Django full-stack web application designed to digitize dental clinic operations — from patient registration and appointment scheduling to treatment tracking, billing, and admin analytics. Deployed on Vercel with Neon PostgreSQL.

Challenge

Traditional dental clinics rely on paper records and manual appointment booking, leading to scheduling conflicts, lost patient data, and inefficient billing. The challenge was to build a modern, secure, and user-friendly digital system that could handle the complete patient lifecycle within a single platform.

Solution

Patient Management

CRUD operations for patient records with medical history, allergies, and treatment notes stored in PostgreSQL.

Appointment Scheduling

Calendar-based appointment booking with conflict detection, reminder notifications, and doctor availability slots.

Billing & Invoicing

Automated invoice generation, treatment cost tracking, payment status management, and financial reporting.

Admin Dashboard

Analytics dashboard with patient statistics, revenue charts, appointment trends, and staff management.

Tech Stack

Django 5.x PostgreSQL Django REST Framework Tailwind CSS WhiteNoise Vercel Serverless Neon DB
